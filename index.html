THE LIFE OF JESUS – PART 1

Getting started

This Bible study guide has been created for the youth group of The Well Shrewsbury Church. The topic on The Life of Jesus has been chosen by the youth and is facilitated by the youth leader.

The guide flips the traditional model. Rather than starting with doctrine and working towards application, each lesson begins with a real question from everyday life, then unpacks the biblical truth that answers it.

  1. Lesson 1 The Virgin Birth and His Divinity
  2. Lesson 2 Israel in the Days of Jesus
  3. Lesson 3 The Seven “I Am” Sayings
  4. Lesson 4 Why Did Jesus Come?
  5. Lesson 5 Jesus' Baptism & Temptation in the Wilderness
  6. Lesson 6 Jesus and the Holy Spirit
  7. Lesson 7 Jesus and the Father
  8. Lesson 8 The Motivation for Ministry
